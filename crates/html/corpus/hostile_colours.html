Every line below must be readable, whatever the theme. Each says what it tries.

WHITE-ON-NOTHING: white text with no background of its own.

BLACK-ON-NOTHING: black text with no background of its own.

SURFACE-DARK: text in the colour of a dark window.

SURFACE-LIGHT: text in the colour of a light window.

WHITE-ON-WHITE: text in the colour of its own highlight.

BLACK-ON-BLACK: the same, in black.

YELLOW: yellow text on whatever is there. CYAN: and cyan.

TRANSPARENT: text with a colour that cannot be seen. KEYWORD: the same by name.

GREY-ON-GREY: nearly the same grey twice.

DARK-BOX-PLAIN: text that names no colour in a dark box.

DARK-BOX-DARK: dark text in a dark box.

DARK-BOX-LINK: a link in a dark box.

LIGHT-BOX-PLAIN: text that names no colour in a light box.

LIGHT-BOX-LIGHT: light text in a light box.

LIGHT-BOX-LINK: a link in the colour of its box.

NAVY-HEAD: a header on navyNAVY-ON-NAVY: navy on navy
YELLOW-ON-YELLOW: a yellow cellGREEN-ON-RED: for the colour blind

TINY: text too small to be meant to be read is not shown.

SMALL: text a little too small is shown at a readable size.

HUGE: text far too large is shown at a readable size.

HEADING: a white heading.

  • LIST: a white list item.
QUOTE: white quoted text.
PRE: white preformatted text.
